Kim Chaewon is a highly talented South Korean singer and the charismatic leader of the popular K-pop girl group LE SSERAFIM. Before her debut with LE SSERAFIM in 2022, she gained widespread recognition and a dedicated global fanbase as a member of the project group IZ*ONE. Known for her distinctive, honey-like vocal tone and sharp dancing skills, she consistently delivers captivating performances on stage. Offstage, her bright personality and iconic short haircut have made her a prominent fashion icon and a favorite among fans worldwide.
